# PitSense Team & Credits

## Team

**Abhinav Gupta and Team**

PitSense is developed collaboratively by **Abhinav Gupta and the PitSense team** as an engineering-focused motorsport intelligence and race-engineering decision-support project.

## Project

> **From radio to race-winning decisions.**

PitSense combines driver-radio perception, multimodal emotion analysis, explainable stress scoring, telemetry context, temporal reasoning, deterministic race-engineering decisions, and an optional AI language layer.

## Credit & Attribution
